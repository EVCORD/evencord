# EvenCord 1.1.3

Adds 42 compatible custom plugins from [Esharq](https://github.com/LOSTSTR/Esharq), with the original Esharq logo, author credits and source links. Use **Show Esharq** in the plugin filters to find them. New additions are off until you enable them.

Esharq updates are checked every six hours alongside Equicord and published through EvenCord updates after a successful build. Shared Equicord plugins and your existing PasscodeLock stay in place.
