# EvenCord 1.1.8

EvenClean now appears as **حذف الرسائل** in its window, toolbar and controls. Confirm a reviewed batch with a checkbox and the Delete button, without typing a phrase or count.

Message search now has a 90-second budget, excluding manual pauses, with shorter local pauses between pages and filters sent with the search. Partial results remain available if the search cannot finish. Stop and Review no longer wait for a pending search response; late search results cannot overwrite the preview. Discord-requested cooldowns remain respected, and unresolved deletion requests still block overlapping work.

The quote window now displays **Quoter Enhanced | الاقتباس المطوّر**. Its plugin card and settings credit the original Equicord Quoter and the Valenced Quoter design reference, with source links in settings and Discord.
